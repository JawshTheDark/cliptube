The first ClipTube app for Android.

**Install:** download the `.apk` below on your phone and open it. Android asks once to allow installs from your browser or file manager. Later versions install over this one and keep your sign-ins.

**Sign-in needs ClipTube 1.5 or newer on the server.** Browsing and watching work on any version.

What's in it:

- Home feed with the website's tabs: All, This site, For you, Subscriptions, Trending, Vertical, Top rated and popular tags.
- Watch clips in the native player, with fullscreen, picture-in-picture, resume where you left off, likes, subscribe, share and comments with replies.
- Clips from the rest of the ClipTube network play from their own server, and you can like, comment and subscribe through yours.
- Search, the game catalog with SteamGridDB artwork, game pages and channel pages.
- Upload clips from your phone with title, description, game, tags and visibility, with progress and processing status.
- Your clips (edit details, publish drafts, delete), history, liked clips, subscriptions and notifications with an unread badge.
- Several servers: switch between clips.irc.so, the other servers in the network or any ClipTube address, with a sign-in kept for each.
- Links to clips.irc.so, clips.quitlyingto.us and clips.6697.pro can open in the app.
