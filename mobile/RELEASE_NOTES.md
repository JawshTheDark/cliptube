ClipTube for Android 1.1.0 catches up with ClipTube 1.6: linked accounts.

**Install:** download the `.apk` below on your phone and open it. It installs over 1.0.0 and keeps your servers and sign-ins.

What's new:

- **Linked accounts** under You: link your accounts on other ClipTube servers or Mastodon, link back the ones that say they're you, check links again, unlink, and copy your handle for the other side.
- **Channel pages** show **Also on** with the channel's linked accounts. Accounts on servers in your network open in the app.
- **Subscribe to all** subscribes to a channel and follows its linked channels elsewhere in one tap.
- An **All accounts** filter on channel pages brings in the channel's videos from its other servers.
- **Comments** from the uploader's linked accounts carry the uploader's mark.
- Too many failed sign-ins (ClipTube 1.6.1) shows the server's "try again in a few minutes" message.

Linked accounts need ClipTube 1.6 or newer on the server; everything else works from 1.5.
