OurTube for Android 1.4.1: one video at a time.

**Install:** download the `.apk` below on your phone and open it. It installs over earlier versions and keeps your servers and sign-ins.

- **Opening another video pauses the one you were watching.** Before, the first video kept playing underneath. Going Back to it picks it up where it stopped. Live streams and watch parties count as videos too.
- **Comments, Save and channel pages leave the video playing,** so you can read the comments while it plays.
- The error for an address that isn't an OurTube server now reads "an OurTube server".
