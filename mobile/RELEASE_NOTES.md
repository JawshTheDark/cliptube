OurTube for Android 1.6.0: turns with your device, made for tablets, and a Density setting.

**Install:** download the `.apk` below on your phone or tablet and open it. It installs over earlier versions and keeps your servers and sign-ins.

- **The app turns.** Before, it stayed upright. Now it follows the device into landscape, and the video keeps playing where it was when you turn it.
- **A widescreen layout** for tablets and phones on their side. The watch page puts the player and its details on the left and more videos on the right. Live streams and watch parties put the video on the left and the chat on the right.
- **More videos across.** Home, channels, games and Shorts fill the width: up to four cards across a tablet on its side, and more Shorts. Forms and settings stay a comfortable width in the middle.
- **Density** (You → App settings): *Comfortable*, *Compact* or *Dense*, the same as the website's. Each step makes the cards and their text smaller, and more fit across, so you scroll less. *Dense* shows two videos across a phone held upright, and up to six across a tablet.
