ClipTube for Android 1.2.0: your main server, playlists, watch parties and live streams.

**Install:** download the `.apk` below on your phone and open it. It installs over earlier versions and keeps your servers and sign-ins.

What's new:

- **Your main server.** The first time you open the app, you pick your server instead of starting on clips.irc.so. The app opens on your main server every time; change it under **You → App settings** or with **Make main** on the Servers screen. Updating keeps the server you were last on as your main one.
- **Playlists and Watch later** (ClipTube 1.7+). Save any clip, including ones from other servers, to Watch later or a playlist. Play a list through with shuffle, loop and autoplay, edit and reorder your own, and see channels' public playlists on their pages.
- **Watch parties** (ClipTube 1.8+). **Watch together** on a clip or a playlist starts a party. Everyone sees the same moment, with chat, reactions and a shared queue. The host steers, or lets everyone steer, and others can step out and back in sync. Friends without an account join under a name.
- **Live streams** (ClipTube 1.9+). Home shows who's live on your server and across the network, and live channels get a LIVE banner. The live page plays the stream with live chat and reactions; chat signed in or under a name. Streamers and moderators can remove messages and ban from the chat.
- Party, playlist and live links from clips.irc.so, clips.quitlyingto.us and clips.6697.pro open in the app.
- View counts say "1 view" instead of "1 views".
