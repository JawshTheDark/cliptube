OurTube for Android 1.5.0: a Shorts viewer, and times in comments that jump the video.

**Install:** download the `.apk` below on your phone and open it. It installs over earlier versions and keeps your servers and sign-ins.

- **Shorts open in their own viewer.** One Short fills the screen in a frame of its own shape, so there are no black bars beside it. Swipe up for the next one. The one on screen plays and loops; tap it to pause.
- **Buttons beside each Short:** like (with the count), dislike, comments, share and the full watch page. A mute button sits at the top, and a thin line shows how far along it is.
- **Comments on a Short open as a sheet** over the lower part of the screen, so the Short keeps playing above them.
- **Times in comments are links.** Tap "1:23" or "1:02:03" in a comment, or in a video's description, and the video jumps there.
- **Add time** in the comment box puts the video's current time where the cursor is.
- **Comments on PeerTube videos** now show their whole thread, with names and avatars from PeerTube. Tap someone's name to open their profile.
- Links to Shorts on clips.irc.so, clips.quitlyingto.us and clips.6697.pro open in the app.

The Shorts viewer and the PeerTube threads need OurTube 1.17 on the server; older servers still work.
